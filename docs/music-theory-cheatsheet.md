# Music Theory Cheatsheet

## 1. Note Names

Chromatic notes repeat every 12 semitones:

`C C#/Db D D#/Eb E F F#/Gb G G#/Ab A A#/Bb B`

- `#` means sharp: one semitone higher.
- `b` means flat: one semitone lower.
- `E` to `F` and `B` to `C` are already one semitone apart.
- One octave = 12 semitones.

## 3. Major Scale

Formula:

`W W H W W W H`

Example: C major

`C D E F G A B C`

- `W` = whole step = 2 semitones
- `H` = half step = 1 semitone
- Scale degrees: `1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8`
- Main functions: `1` tonic, `4` subdominant, `5` dominant.

## 4. Natural Minor Scale

Formula:

`W H W W H W W`

Example: A natural minor

`A B C D E F G A`

Minor scale degrees compared to major:

`1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7`

## 6. Chord Basics

Chords are built by stacking thirds.

Triads:

| Chord | Formula | Example from C |
|---|---|---|
| Major | `1 3 5` | C E G |
| Minor | `1 b3 5` | C Eb G |
| Diminished | `1 b3 b5` | C Eb Gb |
| Augmented | `1 3 #5` | C E G# |

Seventh chords:

| Chord | Formula | Example from C |
|---|---|---|
| Major 7 | `1 3 5 7` | C E G B |
| Dominant 7 | `1 3 5 b7` | C E G Bb |
| Minor 7 | `1 b3 5 b7` | C Eb G Bb |
| Minor major 7 | `1 b3 5 7` | C Eb G B |
| Half-diminished | `1 b3 b5 b7` | C Eb Gb Bb |
| Diminished 7 | `1 b3 b5 bb7` | C Eb Gb A |

## 7. Diatonic Chords

Major key:
`I ii iii IV V vi vii°`

In C major:

`C Dm Em F G Am Bdim`

Natural minor key:
`i ii° bIII iv v bVI bVII`

In A minor:

`Am Bdim C Dm Em F G`

## 8. Common Progressions

| Progression | Sound / Use |
|---|---|
| `I V vi IV` | Pop, anthemic |
| `vi IV I V` | Pop, emotional |
| `ii V I` | Jazz resolution |
| `I IV V` | Blues, rock, folk |
| `i bVI bVII` | Minor rock / cinematic |
| `i iv v` | Natural minor |
| `i iv V` | Minor with stronger dominant |

## 9. Rhythm Basics

| Note | Beats |
|---|---:|
| Whole | 4 |
| Half | 2 |
| Quarter | 1 |
| Eighth | 1/2 |
| Sixteenth | 1/4 |

Time signatures:

- `4/4`: four quarter-note beats per bar.
- `3/4`: three quarter-note beats per bar.
- `6/8`: two groups of three eighth notes.

## 10. Key Signatures

Circle of fifths, sharps:

`C G D A E B F# C#`

Sharps added in order:

`F# C# G# D# A# E# B#`

Circle of fifths, flats:

`C F Bb Eb Ab Db Gb Cb`

Flats added in order:

`Bb Eb Ab Db Gb Cb Fb`

Relative minor:

- The relative minor starts on scale degree `6` of the major key.
- Example: C major -> A minor.

## 11. MIDI Reference

- Middle C is usually `C4`, MIDI note `60`.
- Standard MIDI drums are usually on channel `10`.
- Velocity controls note intensity: `1` to `127`.
- Pitch bend and modulation are separate MIDI controls.

## 12. Fast Songwriting Workflow

1. Pick a key.
2. Pick a scale: major, natural minor, or pentatonic.
3. Build chords from the scale.
4. Start with a simple progression like `I V vi IV`.
5. Write a melody using chord tones on strong beats.
6. Add passing notes between chord tones.
7. Use rhythm and repetition to make the idea memorable.
