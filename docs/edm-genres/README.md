## Universal Production Tips

- **Gain staging**: Keep channel levels around -18 dBFS RMS to leave headroom.
- **Sidechain compression**: Essential in nearly all EDM genres — pump the bass and pads against the kick.
- **Frequency layering**: Layer sounds across the spectrum; avoid frequency clashes with EQ.
- **Automation**: Movement is what makes electronic music feel alive — automate filters, volumes, and FX.
- **Reference tracks**: Always A/B your mix against professionally mastered tracks in the same genre.
- **Mono compatibility**: Check your mix in mono frequently to catch phase issues.
