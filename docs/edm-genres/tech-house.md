# Tech House Production Cheat Sheet

## 1. Core Genre Characteristics

Tech House sits between Techno and House:
- **Groove-focused** — the rhythm is king; everything serves the groove
- **Minimal melody** — sparse, functional melodic elements
- **Techy, industrial sounds** — mechanical synths, processed percussion
- **Driving bassline** — the bass and groove are inseparable
- **Hypnotic repetition** — loops and subtle automation create movement

The goal is a groove so deep and hypnotic that the dancefloor never stops moving.

---

## 2. Tempo

| Style | BPM |
|---|---|
| Classic Tech House | 126–130 |
| Modern Tech House | 128–132 |
| Hard Tech House | 130–136 |

**Recommended starting point:** 130 BPM.

---

## 3. Song Structure

Tech house arrangements are long and loop-based.

| Section | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 32–64 bars | Kick + groove only; DJ-friendly |
| Main Groove A | 32–64 bars | Full groove; bassline enters |
| Break | 16–32 bars | Subtle strip-down; FX builds |
| Main Groove B | 32–64 bars | Variation; new element introduced |
| Peak Section | 32 bars | All elements in; tension high |
| Outro | 32–64 bars | Gradual strip-down |

**Typical total length:** 7–10 minutes.

---

## 4. Kick Drum

### Characteristics
- Punchy transient with solid low-end (60–80 Hz)
- Longer sustain than house but shorter than techno
- Minimal "tail" — clean and tight
- Often layered: transient click + sub body

### Pattern
```
K . . . K . . . K . . . K . . .
(4-on-the-floor; no swing)
```

### Processing
- Hard sidechain against bass and pads (LFO Tool)
- Transient shaper to control punch
- Mild saturation for character

---

## 5. Bass

The bassline in tech house IS the hook.

### Characteristics
- Driving, repetitive phrase — 1–4 bars that loops
- Sub-bass with mid-range presence (100–300 Hz growl)
- Groove-locked to kick with subtle syncopation
- Often with a filter that slightly opens/closes

### Common Sounds
- **Distorted 909 bass** — the signature tech house sound
- **Reese bass** — detuned, dark, droning
- **Pluck bass** — short attack, punchy
- **Pitched up 808** — sub with character

### Example Pattern (1 bar, 16th notes)
```
A . . A . . A A . . A . . A . .
(driving 16th with off-beat doubles)
```

---

## 6. Groove Elements

Tech house is built around **percussion loops**, not melody.

### Drum Machine Elements
| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| Kick | 4/4 foundation |
| Clap / Snare | Beats 2 and 4; minimal |
| Hi-Hat | 16th note groove — essential |
| Ride | Adds forward motion |
| Open Hat | Sporadic, adds groove |
| Rim Shot | Syncopated accents |

### Percussion Samples
- Industrial textures — metallic, clicky
- Finger snaps, woodblocks, electronic toms
- Short, dry — no reverb on percussion (or very tight)

---

## 7. Melodic Elements

Tech house intentionally keeps melody minimal.

### Synth Stabs
- Short, punchy chord stabs on offbeats
- Heavily filtered — often low-passed
- Mono or narrow stereo

### Bass Hooks
- The bass itself carries the "melodic" identity
- A short 2–4 note riff repeated hypnotically

### Atmospherics
- Long, filtered pad underneath (very low in the mix)
- White/pink noise texture for energy
- Spoken word / vocal samples (short, chopped)

---

## 8. Vocals

- **Vocal samples** — chopped, pitched, and repeated as rhythmic elements
- Short phrases: "yeah", "jack", "work it", "ugh" — used percussively
- Heavy processing: pitch shift, bit-crush, filter, reverb
- Rarely full vocal lines

---

## 9. FX & Automation

Tech house lives and dies by automation.

| Element | Automation Target |
|---|---|
| Bass | Filter cutoff — subtle open/close on 8-bar cycles |
| Hi-hat | Volume, pitch, or decay |
| Synth stabs | Filter, distortion amount |
| Pads | Volume swells |
| Reverb | Pre-delay or size sweeps |

### Must-Have FX
- **LFO Tool / Volume Shaper** for sidechain pump
- **Distortion / Saturation** on bass and kick
- **Delay** (8th or 16th note) on percussion fills
- **Reverb** (short room) on claps

---

## 10. Mixdown Tips

- **Punch**: Parallel compression on the drum bus
- **Bass**: High-pass bass at ~30 Hz; cut mud around 250 Hz
- **Groove**: Ensure kick and bass are phase-aligned
- **Energy**: The mix should feel aggressive and forward
- **Loudness target**: -8 to -10 LUFS for club play
