# House Music Production Cheat Sheet

## 1. Core Genre Characteristics

House music is built around:
- **4/4 kick drum** — steady, driving pulse
- **Soulful, funky groove** — swing in the rhythm, feel in the hats
- **Vocal chops and hooks** — often sampled or processed vocals
- **Piano / chord stabs** — short, rhythmic chord hits
- **Bassline** — bouncy, melodic, and groove-driven

The goal is to make people move on the dance floor while feeling uplifted.

---

## 2. Tempo

| Style | BPM |
|---|---|
| Classic / Chicago House | 120–125 |
| Deep House | 120–124 |
| UK House | 124–128 |
| Modern House | 124–130 |

**Recommended starting point:** 124 BPM.

---

## 3. Song Structure

| Section | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 16–32 bars | DJ-friendly; minimal groove |
| Verse / Groove | 16–32 bars | Main groove, bassline, hats |
| Pre-Chorus / Build | 8–16 bars | Filter opens, energy rises |
| Chorus / Hook | 16–32 bars | Vocal hook, full energy |
| Bridge / Break | 16 bars | Stripped back, tension |
| Drop / Second chorus | 32 bars | Full energy return |
| Outro | 16–32 bars | Elements strip away |

**Typical total length:** 5–7 minutes.

---

## 4. Kick Drum

### Characteristics
- Punchy with a defined transient click
- Moderate low-end (not as deep as techno)
- Clean, tight tail — typically 200–400 ms
- Often sampled from classic drum machines (Roland TR-909, TR-808)

### Pattern
```
K . . . K . . . K . . . K . . .
(4-on-the-floor)
```

### Processing
- Layer a 909-style kick with a low-end sub click
- EQ out muddiness around 250 Hz
- Sidechain compress bass and pads against kick

---

## 5. Bass

### Characteristics
- Melodic, bouncy bassline — follows chord root with passing notes
- Warm, slightly distorted character
- Often layered: sub sine + mid punch
- Groove-locked with the kick and hats

### Classic Bassline Pattern (1 bar, 16th notes)
```
Root . . . 5th . . Root . Third . Root . . .
```

### Synth Choices
- **Moog-style filter** (Sylenth1, Diva, or Model D) for warmth
- **Roland TB-303 style** for acid house — resonant filter sweeps
- Keep sub below 100 Hz, mid punch 100–300 Hz

---

## 6. Chord Progressions

House often uses **minor** and **major 7th chords** for warmth and soul.

| Progression | Example (A minor) | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| i – IV – VII – III | Am – Dm – G – C | Funky, uplifting |
| I – IV – ii – V | Cmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Dm7 – G7 | Jazzy, deep |
| i – VI – III – VII | Am – F – C – G | Classic house |
| I – vi – IV – V | C – Am – F – G | Gospel-influenced |

---

## 7. Melody / Stabs

### Piano / Chord Stabs
- Short, punchy — played on offbeats (16th note syncopation)
- Upright piano or electric piano sound (Rhodes, Wurli)
- Usually 3–4 note voicings

### Stab Pattern Example
```
. . . C . . . C . F . . . C . .
(offbeat hits)
```

### Synth Pads
- Lush, warm background pads under the stabs
- Slow attack, long release
- Subtle movement via LFO on filter

---

## 8. Drums & Percussion

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| Kick | TR-909 or similar; 4-on-the-floor |
| Clap / Snare | Beats 2 and 4; TR-909 clap is iconic |
| Hi-Hat (closed) | 8th or 16th note groove with swing |
| Hi-Hat (open) | Offbeats, adds a loose feel |
| Ride | Optional for extra groove |
| Shaker | Often layered for feel |
| Conga / Bongo | Subtle Latin-influenced percussion layers |

### Swing
Apply **50–60% swing** to hi-hats and percussion for that classic house groove. The note timing should feel slightly behind or ahead of the grid.

---

## 9. Vocals

- Soulful, gospel-influenced singing
- Vocal chops and stabs (short slices played rhythmically)
- **Sampled diva vocals** are a house staple
- Often run through a light compression + EQ + reverb chain
- Pitch-correct subtly if needed (not robotic like EDM)

---

## 10. FX Elements

| FX | Usage |
|---|---|
| Filter sweep | Classic rising filter on synths before chorus |
| Reverb | Room/hall on clap, long on vocal |
| Delay | 1/8 or 1/4 note delay on synths, vocals |
| Phaser / Flanger | On chord stabs or bass for movement |
| Vinyl crackle | Adds warmth in deep/classic house |

---

## 11. Mixdown Tips

- **Drums**: Keep kick and bass tight in the low end; sidechain
- **Groove**: Use swing/humanize on hats; avoid a robotic feel
- **Warmth**: Gentle tape saturation on the master bus
- **Stereo width**: Mono bass/kick, wide pads and chops
- **Loudness target**: -14 LUFS for streaming; -8 to -10 for club

---

## 12. Sub-Genres at a Glance

| Sub-Genre | Key Traits |
|---|---|
| Chicago House | Raw, funky, 909-driven, gospel vocals |
| Deep House | Jazzy chords, subtle groove, introspective |
| Tech House | Minimal, techy sounds, groove-focused |
| Progressive House | Long builds, melodic, flowing |
| Afro House | African percussion, organic textures |
| Slap House | Modern pop crossover, slapping bass |
| Funky House | Disco-influenced, funky guitars and bass |
