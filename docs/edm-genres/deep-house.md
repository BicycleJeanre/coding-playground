# Deep House Production Cheat Sheet

## 1. Core Genre Characteristics

Deep House is characterized by:
- **Warmth and depth** — low-register sounds, smooth textures
- **Jazzy chord voicings** — 7ths, 9ths, and extended chords
- **Subtle, hypnotic groove** — restrained energy, not anthemic
- **Introspective mood** — emotional without being euphoric
- **Organic-feeling elements** — vinyl texture, live-sounding instruments

The goal is intimacy, movement, and atmosphere — a late-night feeling.

---

## 2. Tempo

| Style | BPM |
|---|---|
| Classic Deep House | 118–122 |
| Modern Deep House | 120–126 |
| Soulful Deep House | 116–122 |

**Recommended starting point:** 122 BPM.

---

## 3. Song Structure

| Section | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 32 bars | Very minimal — kick + bass or just atmosphere |
| Groove Intro | 16–32 bars | Full groove, no melody yet |
| Main Section A | 32 bars | Chords and melody introduced |
| Break | 16 bars | Atmosphere only, no drums |
| Main Section B | 32 bars | Slight variation, build |
| Outro | 32 bars | Gradual strip |

**Typical total length:** 6–8 minutes. Deep house loves long, patient arrangements.

---

## 4. Kick Drum

### Characteristics
- Softer than house — less click, more thud
- Vintage drum machine character (TR-909, but less sharp)
- Shorter tail than techno
- Often slightly swung

### Pattern
```
K . . . K . . . K . . . K . . .
(4-on-the-floor, but subtly swung)
```

### Processing
- Gentle compression — keep natural feel
- Slight saturation for warmth
- Low-cut on everything competing below 80 Hz

---

## 5. Bass

### Characteristics
- Deep, warm sub bass — smooth and round
- Follows the root of each chord
- Moves slowly — often whole or half notes with occasional movement
- Vintage bass synth or even real bass guitar

### Synth Choices
- Moog-style bass (Diva, Model D)
- Rhodes bass patch
- Real bass guitar for organic feel

### Bassline Example (4 bars, slow movement)
```
Bar 1: Am (root A, long sustained)
Bar 2: Am (root A with passing D)
Bar 3: Dm (root D, sustained)
Bar 4: G (root G, slight descend)
```

---

## 6. Chord Progressions

Deep house relies heavily on **jazz-influenced chords**.

| Progression | Example | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| i7 – IV9 – VII7 – III7 | Am7 – Dmaj9 – G7 – Cmaj7 | Classic jazzy deep |
| Imaj7 – iii7 – vi7 – IVmaj7 | Cmaj7 – Em7 – Am7 – Fmaj7 | Warm, smooth |
| i9 – VII9 – VI9 – VII9 | Am9 – G9 – Fmaj9 – G9 | Late-night, melancholic |

- Use lots of **sus2**, **sus4**, and **add9** extensions
- Voice chords in mid-range (not too low, not too shrill)
- Change chords every 4–8 bars for a hypnotic effect

---

## 7. Melody / Pads

### Keys / Rhodes
- Electric piano (Rhodes, Wurlitzer) is a deep house staple
- Short chord stabs or long sustained pads
- Subtly detuned for warmth
- Tape saturation on the Rhodes sound

### Pads
- Slow attack, very long release
- Lush reverb (large hall)
- No sharp transients

### Lead Melody (optional)
- Subtle, understated — doesn't dominate
- Saxophone, flute, or smooth synth
- Sparse — lots of space and breathing room

---

## 8. Drums & Percussion

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| Kick | Soft, warm — TR-909 with less EQ bite |
| Snare / Clap | Beats 2 and 4; airy and roomy |
| Hi-Hat (closed) | 8th or 16th groove — **swing is essential** |
| Hi-Hat (open) | Loose, relaxed offbeat |
| Rim | Subtle 16th-note fills |
| Shaker | Gentle groove layer |
| Bongo / Conga | Sparse, hand-drumming feel |

### Swing Amount
Use **55–65% swing** — the groove should feel relaxed and human, never mechanical.

---

## 9. Vocals

- **Low-mix, intimate vocals** — not upfront
- Spoken word, whispered phrases, or soul snippets
- Heavy reverb — vocals blend into the texture
- Often from old soul/gospel samples (with clearance!)

---

## 10. Atmosphere & FX

| FX | Usage |
|---|---|
| Vinyl noise / crackle | Under the whole track for warmth |
| Long reverb | On pads, snare, vocals |
| Tape delay | Subtle slap on piano chords |
| Filter | Very slow, subtle modulation |
| Room ambience | Mild room on drums for realness |

---

## 11. Mixdown Tips

- **Less is more** — resist the urge to fill every frequency
- **Warmth**: Tape saturation plugin on groups (Waves J37, Softube Tape)
- **Stereo width**: Slightly narrower than big-room house; intimate feel
- **Dynamic range**: Keep it slightly more dynamic — not crushed
- **Loudness target**: -14 to -16 LUFS; deep house breathes
