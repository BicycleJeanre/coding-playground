# Hardstyle Production Cheat Sheet

## 1. Core Genre Characteristics

Hardstyle is built around:
- **The Hardstyle kick** — distorted, reverse-bass, punishing
- **Euphoric leads** — soaring, emotional, anthemic melodies
- **Driving energy** — unrelenting pace, no compromise
- **Distorted bass tail** — the "reverse bass" under the kick
- **Contrast** — brutal drops vs emotional breakdowns

The philosophy: raw power meets emotional euphoria.

---

## 2. Tempo

| Style | BPM |
|---|---|
| Classic Hardstyle | 148–152 |
| Euphoric Hardstyle | 150–156 |
| Hard Trance (related) | 148–155 |
| Rawstyle | 148–155 |
| Hardcore (related) | 160–180 |

**Recommended starting point:** 150 BPM.

---

## 3. Song Structure

| Section | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 16–32 bars | Kick and minimal elements |
| Groove | 16–32 bars | Full beat established |
| Melody Intro | 16 bars | Lead melody enters |
| Breakdown | 16–32 bars | Emotional piano/strings; no kick |
| Pre-Drop Build | 8–16 bars | Rising tension; riser; snare roll |
| Drop | 32 bars | Full kick + bass + lead |
| Second Breakdown | 16 bars | Often more intense emotionally |
| Second Drop | 32 bars | Same or harder variation |
| Outro | 16–32 bars | Strip down |

**Typical total length:** 5–7 minutes.

## 4. The Hardstyle Kick

This is the most iconic element of hardstyle. Getting it right is the art.

### Components
1. **Body/Punch**: A very short, distorted sine or saw layer — the "thud"
2. **Reverse bass**: A reversed low-frequency element that swells *into* the kick
3. **Distortion**: The kick is heavily distorted (waveshaper, overdrive)
4. **Pitch sweep**: Fast downward pitch drop on impact (200 Hz → 50 Hz in ~80 ms)

### Building the Kick (Step by Step)
1. Start with a **kick sample** or synthesized sine with punch
2. Apply heavy **waveshaper / distortion** to create harmonic content
3. Layer a **reverse bass** (reversed sub) so the tail precedes the click
4. Shape the envelope: very fast attack, short decay (~200–400 ms)
5. Tune to the root note (e.g. A at 55 Hz for A minor)
6. EQ: High-pass the mud; boost ~80 Hz for sub; cut 200–400 Hz

### Pattern
```
K . . . K . . . K . . . K . . .
(4-on-the-floor; no swing; metronomic)
```

---

## 5. Bass

### Characteristics
- The "bass" in hardstyle is mostly the kick's own reverse bass tail
- Additional bassline is minimal — often just a sub sine following the root
- Sub lives below 80 Hz; very mono

### Bassline
- Simple: root note of each chord, sustained
- Transitions between chords on the bar change

---

## 6. Chord Progressions

Hardstyle typically uses **minor keys** for power and emotion.

| Progression | Example (A minor) | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| i – VI – III – VII | Am – F – C – G | Classic euphoric |
| i – VII – VI – VII | Am – G – F – G | Anthemic, driving |
| i – iv – VII – III | Am – Dm – G – C | Melancholic, epic |

- Chords play on 4-bar or 8-bar cycles
- Often on piano, organ, or lush strings in the breakdown

---

## 7. Melody / Leads

### Breakdown
- Emotional piano or string melody — the emotional core
- Sweeping strings, choir, and pads
- Often uses arpeggiated patterns
- Long reverb tails

### Drop Lead
- Supersaw or square-wave lead
- Distorted slightly at the high end
- Unison voices detuned for massive width
- Sidechain to kick (ducking during the kick transient)

### Breakdown Instruments
| Instrument | Role |
|---|---|
| Grand piano | Melody and chords |
| Strings | Pads and harmonic support |
| Choir | Atmospheric swell |
| Synth pad | Background warmth |
| Pluck synth | Melodic counter-line |

---

## 8. Drums & Percussion

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| Kick | The star — distorted, reverse bass |
| Clap / Snare | Beats 2 and 4; loud and sharp |
| Hi-Hat | 8th note pattern; mechanical |
| Open Hat | Offbeats for energy |
| Cymbal | Crash on important downbeats |
| Percussion | Minimal — kick dominates |

---

## 9. Builds & Breakdowns

### Breakdown Must-Haves
- Piano melody (often the main hook)
- String swells
- Reverb everything
- No kick (or sparse minimal kick)
- Emotional peak of the track

### Pre-Drop Build
- Rising white noise sweep
- Snare roll (16th note fill building intensity)
- Filter opening on synths
- Riser FX
- Reverse reverb of the drop lead
- Last 2 bars: stutter or cut effect for tension

---

## 10. FX Elements

| FX | Usage |
|---|---|
| Riser / Sweep | 8–16 bar rising FX into drop |
| Reverse cymbal | Last 2 bars before drop |
| Snare roll | Final 2–4 bars building to drop |
| Impact / Hit | Downbeat of the drop |
| Distortion | On kick; on lead synths |
| Reverb | Heavy on breakdown elements |
| Delay | Lead synth in the drop |

---

## 11. Mixdown Tips

- **Kick**: Must dominate the low end; everything else gets out of the way
- **Sidechain**: Aggressive pumping on bass and pads vs kick
- **Leads**: High-pass at ~200 Hz; boost highs for brilliance
- **Breakdown**: Reverb heavy — the contrast with the dry kick drop is what makes it hit
- **Loudness target**: -6 to -9 LUFS; hardstyle is mastered loud for festival systems

---

## 12. Sub-Genres at a Glance

| Sub-Genre | Key Traits |
|---|---|
| Euphoric Hardstyle | Emotional breakdowns, soaring leads, melodic |
| Rawstyle | Darker, dirtier kick, less melody, aggressive |
| Hard Trance | Trance-influenced, less extreme kick, more melody |
| Hardcore | Even faster (160+), gabber kicks, extreme aggression |
| Big Room Hardstyle | Festival-ready, huge leads, simplified structure |
