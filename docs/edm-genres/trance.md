# Trance Music Production Cheat Sheet

## 1. Core Genre Characteristics

Trance is built around:
- **Hypnotic repetition** — looping patterns that induce a trance-like state
- **Emotional melodies** — soaring leads and arpeggios that carry the emotional weight
- **Driving rhythm** — relentless 4/4 kick with energetic percussion layers
- **Layered synth textures** — stacked pads, leads, and arpeggios building tension
- **Gradual progression and tension/release** — breakdowns followed by climactic drops

The goal is to create momentum and emotional payoff.

---

## 2. Tempo

| Style | BPM |
|---|---|
| Progressive Trance | 126–132 |
| Uplifting Trance | 134–140 |
| Psytrance | 138–145 |
| Classic / Melodic Trance | 130–138 |

**Recommended starting point:** 138 BPM for uplifting trance.

---

## 3. Song Structure

| Section | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 16–32 bars | DJ-friendly entry; kick + minimal elements |
| Groove Build | 16 bars | Full groove introduced; filter opens up |
| Main Build | 16–32 bars | Tension builds; melodic elements introduced |
| Breakdown | 16–32 bars | Drums drop; atmosphere + emotional peak |
| Climax / Drop | 32 bars | Full energy; all elements in |
| Second Build | 16 bars | Brief re-tension before second drop |
| Second Drop | 32 bars | Often a slight variation |
| Outro | 16–32 bars | Elements strip away; DJ-friendly exit |

**Typical total length:** 6–9 minutes.

---

## 4. Kick Drum

### Characteristics
- Deep low-end punch (around 60–80 Hz fundamental)
- Tight, clean transient — not too long
- Tuned to the root note of the track
- High-pass the top of the kick to make room for subs

### Pattern
Classic **4-on-the-floor**:
```
K . . . K . . . K . . . K . . .
(every beat, 1/4 notes)
```

### Processing
- Layer two kicks: one for low-end body, one for click/transient
- Transient shaper to tighten attack
- Sidechain everything (bass, pads) to the kick

---

## 5. Bass

### Sub Bass
- Sustained sine or slightly detuned sawtooth
- Lives mostly below 100–120 Hz
- Sidechained tightly to the kick (ducking 6–12 dB)
- Tuned to the root note or follows chord root

### Mid Bass / Bass Lead
- In uplifting trance, a rolling bassline using 16th notes is common
- Often a filtered saw or acid-style bass
- Modulate cutoff frequency with automation or LFO for movement

### Pattern Example (Key of A minor)
```
A  A  A  A  E  E  G  G  (following the chord progression)
```

---

## 6. Chord Progressions

Trance favors **minor keys** for emotion. Common progressions:

| Progression | Example (A minor) | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| i – VI – III – VII | Am – F – C – G | Classic uplifting |
| i – VII – VI – VII | Am – G – F – G | Driving, anthemic |
| i – iv – VII – III | Am – Dm – G – C | Melancholic, deep |
| i – VI – VII – i | Am – F – G – Am | Dark, resolving |

- Chords are usually played on 4-bar or 8-bar cycles
- Use **inversions** and **voice leading** for smooth transitions

---

## 7. Melody / Leads

### Characteristics
- Soaring, emotional phrases — often stepwise with occasional large leaps
- Pentatonic minor or natural minor scales
- 4–16 bar melodic phrases
- Peak in the breakdown and drop sections

### Synth Choices
| Sound | Synth / Technique |
|---|---|
| Supersaw lead | Serum / Sylenth1 — stack 7+ unison voices, slight detune |
| Pluck lead | Short attack, fast decay, resonant filter sweep |
| Emotional pad | Lush reverb, slow attack, moderate release |
| Trance gate pad | Pad + trance gate LFO (16th or 8th note rhythm) |

### Tips
- Add a long reverb tail to the lead in the breakdown
- Sidechain the lead subtly to the kick in the drop for pump
- Automate the filter cutoff to open up as the drop hits

---

## 8. Arpeggios

Arpeggios are a signature trance element.

### Characteristics
- Usually 16th notes or triplets at high speed
- Follows the chord progression
- Filtered and processed to sit under the lead
- Common synth: Spire, Sylenth1, or Serum with arpeggiator

### Example Pattern (1/16th at 138 BPM)
```
Am arp:  A4 C5 E5 A5 C5 E5 A5 C5 ...
```

### Processing
- High-pass at ~200 Hz to keep out of the bass
- Short reverb, stereo width
- Subtle side-chain to kick

---

## 9. Percussion

| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| Clap / Snare | Beats 2 and 4 (or a rolling clap build) |
| Open Hi-Hat | Offbeats (8th note feel) |
| Closed Hi-Hat | 16th note groove fill |
| Ride | Optional — adds groove in main sections |
| Percussion loops | Shaker, bongo, conga layers for groove |

### Percussion Automation
- Strip percussion during breakdown
- Gradually re-introduce in build-up (e.g. clap rolls, snare fills)
- Build tension with snare roll / clap build in the final 4 bars before drop

---

## 10. FX Elements

| FX | Usage |
|---|---|
| Risers | 8–16 bar slow upward sweep before drops |
| Impacts | Big hit/crash on the drop downbeat |
| Downlifters | Used entering a breakdown |
| Reverse cymbal | Final 1–2 bars before drop |
| White noise sweep | Classic trance build element |
| Stutter / gate | 16th note gate on pads for rhythmic energy |

---

## 11. Mixdown & Mastering Tips

### EQ
- High-pass everything except kick and bass
- Cut 200–400 Hz on synths to reduce muddiness
- Air shelf boost (10 kHz+) on leads for sparkle

### Compression
- Bus compression on the drum group for glue (2–4 dB GR)
- Sidechain bass and pads to kick (LFO Tool or native)

### Stereo
- Keep kick, bass, and sub elements **mono**
- Wide stereo on pads, arpeggios, FX
- Check mono compatibility often

### Loudness Targets
| Platform | Target LUFS |
|---|---|
| Streaming (Spotify, Apple) | -14 LUFS integrated |
| Club / DJ use | -6 to -8 LUFS (louder master) |
| Mastering ceiling | -0.3 dBTP (true peak) |

---

## 12. Sub-Genres at a Glance

| Sub-Genre | Key Traits |
|---|---|
| Uplifting Trance | Big drops, soaring leads, emotional breakdowns |
| Progressive Trance | Slower builds, more groove, subtler drops |
| Psytrance | Acid basslines, psychedelic textures, very fast |
| Tech Trance | Industrial edge, harder kick, darker vibe |
| Vocal Trance | Full vocal hooks, pop structure, radio-friendly |
| Orchestral Trance | Live strings + trance elements, cinematic |
