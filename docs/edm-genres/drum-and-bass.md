# Drum & Bass Production Cheat Sheet

## 1. Core Genre Characteristics

Drum & Bass (DnB) is defined by:
- **Fast, syncopated breakbeats** — complex rhythms at high BPM
- **Massive sub bass** — deep, rolling, or reese bass
- **High energy** — constant drive and forward motion
- **Resampled and chopped breaks** — the Amen break and similar
- **Contrast** — light vs dark, quiet vs loud, choppy breaks vs smooth bass

The goal is kinetic energy — music that physically moves the body.

## 2. Tempo

| Style | BPM |
|---|---|
| Liquid DnB | 172–176 |
| Jump-Up | 172–178 |
| Neurofunk | 174–178 |
| Techstep | 172–176 |
| Jungle (related) | 160–168 |
| Halftime DnB | 85–90 (half-time feel = 170–180) |

**Recommended starting point:** 174 BPM.

---

## 3. Song Structure

| Section | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 16–32 bars | Atmosphere; no breaks yet |
| Intro Drop | 8–16 bars | Breaks enter; hook established |
| Main A | 32 bars | Full energy; break + bass |
| Break / Drop | 8–16 bars | Stripped; atmosphere; snare roll |
| Main B | 32 bars | Reintroduction; variation |
| Bridge | 8 bars | Brief tension build |
| Main C | 32 bars | Intensified variation |
| Outro | 16–32 bars | Gradual strip |

**Typical total length:** 4–6 minutes.

## 4. Drum Programming

This is the most complex part of DnB.

### The Amen Break
The Amen break (from "Amen, Brother" by The Winstons) is the most sampled break in history. The key pattern:
```
Kick: K . . K . . K . K . . K . . K .
Snare: . . S . . S . . . . S . . S . .
Hat:  h h h h h h h h h h h h h h h h
```

### Break Processing
- **Chop and re-arrange**: Slice the break in sampler; rearrange hits
- **Layer**: Multiple break slices layered for texture
- **Transient shaping**: Punch up the kick; brighten the snare
- **Saturation**: Add grit — breaks should feel old and raw
- **Compression**: Parallel compression for punch

### Modern DnB Drums
- Can be fully programmed (not sample-based)
- Kick: deep sub punch (60–80 Hz) with hard transient
- Snare: cracking, bright, aggressive
- Hi-hats: fast 16th/32nd patterns with ghost notes
- Ghost notes and syncopation are essential

### Example Groove (1 bar, 32nd notes at 174 BPM)
```
K . . . . . . . K . S . . K . . K . . . S . . . K . . S . .
(simplified — actual DnB is much more complex)
```

## 5. Bass

Bass in DnB is an art form in itself.

### Types

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Sub bass | Deep, smooth sine — felt more than heard |
| Reese bass | Detuned oscillators; dark, droning, iconic |
| Neuro bass | Heavily modulated; LFOs on filter + pitch |
| Liquid bass | Smooth, melodic; rolls through chord changes |
| Step bass | Choppy, rhythmic; matches break energy |

### Sub Bass Design
- Pure sine wave or slightly saturated sine
- Moves with the kick on beats 1 and 3 typically
- Sidechains subtly to the kick
- Lives entirely below 80–100 Hz

### Neuro Bass Design (Serum/Massive)
- Start with a detuned saw or wavetable
- Add FM modulation
- Map multiple LFOs to: pitch, filter cutoff, amplitude, wavetable position
- Distort and re-filter the result
- Automate LFO rates for dynamic "talking" bass

## 6. Chord Progressions & Melody

DnB is not always melodic. But when it is:

### Liquid DnB
- Full chord progressions; emotional, soulful
- Piano, Rhodes, or warm synth pads
- Vocals (sung, not chopped)
- Common: minor keys, descending bass lines

### Jump-Up / Neurofunk
- Minimal harmony
- Short stabs, rave stabs, resampled chords
- Key: minor, dark

### Common Progressions
| Progression | Feel |
|---|---|
| i – VII – VI – VII | Dark, repeating loop |
| i – III – VII – VI | Classic minor |
| i – iv – i – VII | Moody, anxious |

---

## 7. Pads & Atmosphere

- Tension pads: dissonant, minor, long sustain
- Liquid pads: warm, lush, Rhodes-influenced
- Often heavily reverbed and filtered
- Intro/breakdown is where pads shine

## 8. FX & Sound Design

| FX | Usage |
|---|---|
| Amen chop effects | Pitch shift, reverse, stutter |
| Reese distortion | Drive the bass with saturation |
| Neuro FX | Pitched LFO bass modulation |
| Reverb | On snare, pads, atmosphere |
| Delay | Amen tails, vocal chops |
| Bitcrusher | Gritty drum texture |
| Transient designer | Kick and snare punch |
| Compression (parallel) | Drum punch and glue |

---

## 9. Mixdown Tips

- **Two-tier bass**: Sub (mono, <100 Hz) + Mid bass (mono/narrow, 100–500 Hz)
- **Breaks**: EQ to complement bass — high-pass around 60–80 Hz
- **Kick-bass relationship**: Frequency separate; kick punches where bass breathes
- **Snare**: Bright and cutting — needs to pierce the mix
- **Loudness target**: -8 to -10 LUFS for club play
- **Mono**: Check mix in mono constantly; neuro bass can phase badly

## 10. Sub-Genres at a Glance

| Sub-Genre | Key Traits |
|---|---|
| Liquid DnB | Emotional, soulful, melodic; smooth rolling bass |
| Jump-Up | Fun, bouncy, wobbly bass; dance-floor energy |
| Neurofunk | Dark, complex, technical; modulated neuro bass |
| Techstep | Cold, industrial, robot-like |
| Jungle | Older style; reggae influence, ragga vocals, rawer breaks |
| Halftime | Half-speed groove feel; hip-hop influenced |
| Rollers | Smooth, rolling, groove-focused |
