# Dubstep Production Cheat Sheet

## 1. Core Genre Characteristics

Dubstep is defined by:
- **Half-time feel** — 140 BPM with a 70 BPM groove feel
- **Sub-heavy bass** — devastating low-end is everything
- **Wobble bass** — LFO-modulated bass is the signature sound
- **Space and silence** — the gaps between hits create tension
- **Reggae/dub influence** — origins in UK dub and 2-step garage

There are two main flavors:
- **UK / Original Dubstep** (Skream, Benga) — dark, subtle, minimal
- **Brostep / American Dubstep** (Skrillex era) — aggressive, distorted, maximalist

---

## 2. Tempo

Dubstep is written at 140 BPM but feels like 70 BPM.

| Style | Written BPM | Felt BPM |
|---|---|---|
| UK Dubstep | 138–142 | ~70 |
| Brostep | 140–150 | ~70–75 |
| Riddim | 140–150 | ~70–75 |
| Future Dubstep | 140–150 | ~70 |

**Recommended starting point:** 140 BPM.

---

## 3. Song Structure

| Section | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 16–32 bars | Atmospheric; sub sine + pads |
| Verse | 16–32 bars | Groove established; vocal |
| Pre-Drop Build | 8–16 bars | Rising tension; riser + snare roll |
| Drop | 32 bars | Full wobble bass; maximum energy |
| Break / Interlude | 8–16 bars | Stripped; atmosphere |
| Second Drop | 32 bars | Variation; often heavier than first |
| Outro | 16 bars | Strip down; atmosphere |

**Typical total length:** 3.5–5 minutes.

## 4. Drums

### Half-Time Pattern (the defining feature)
```
Bar 1: K . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
Bar 2: . . . . . . . . S . . . . . . .
(Kick on 1 of every 2 bars; Snare on 3 of every 2 bars)
```

More specifically, in 1/32nd notes at 140 BPM:
```
K . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . S . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
```

### Kick
- Deep, sub-heavy, punchy
- TR-808 kick or custom designed
- Tuned to the key of the track

### Snare
- Loud, prominent, often snappy
- Sometimes pitched or processed
- In brostep: very loud, almost a gunshot

### Hi-Hats
- Sparse — 8th notes at most
- Often adds a rhythmic shuffle
- May include 32nd-note roll before snare

## 5. Bass

### Sub Bass
- A constant, deep sine or triangle wave
- Below 80 Hz; mono always
- Tuned to the root note
- The "weight" that fills the room

### Wobble Bass
The defining dubstep sound. A modulated bass tone.

#### How to Build a Wobble Bass
1. Start with a **sawtooth or super-saw wave**
2. Add a **low-pass filter** with high resonance
3. Set an **LFO** on the filter cutoff
4. LFO rates: 1/4, 1/8, or 1/16 synced to BPM
5. **Automate LFO rate** to vary the wobble speed

#### Common Wubs (Rates)
| LFO Rate | Sound | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1/2 | Slow throb | Intro bass |
| 1/4 | Classic wobble | Standard drop |
| 1/8 | Faster wobble | Builds energy |
| 1/16 | Rapid stutter | Peak tension |

### Growl / Neuro Bass
- FM synthesis with heavy modulation
- Distorted and re-filtered
- "Talks" or growls — formant-like character
- Heavily used in Brostep and Riddim

## 6. Chords & Melody

### Original UK Dubstep
- Dark minor chords
- Reggae/dub-influenced chord progressions
- Spacious atmosphere
- Melody rarely upfront

### Brostep / Modern
- Minimal chord content
- Short stabs or single-note riffs
- Atmosphere from the bass shape itself

### Common Progressions
| Progression | Feel |
|---|---|
| i – VII – VI – VII | Dark, unresolved |
| i – III – iv – VII | Moody, minor |
| i – VI – III – VII | Trance-influenced dubstep |

---

## 7. Atmosphere & Pads

- Dark, cinematic pads
- Dub-influenced: reverb-heavy, echoing
- Sub-bass pads blending into the sub frequency
- Vinyl noise and tape echo textures (UK style)
- Horror film-style ambiences (Brostep)

---

## 8. Vocals

- Deep, reverb-heavy UK grime-style vocals (original dubstep)
- Sampled R&B or soul (female vocal drops)
- Heavy processing: pitch shift, reverb, metallic delay
- Chants and gang vocals in brostep

---

## 9. FX & Sound Design

| FX | Usage |
|---|---|
| LFO Tool | Wobble bass modulation |
| Distortion | Bass saturation; Brostep aggression |
| Reverb | Deep, long — dub-influenced |
| Tape echo | UK dubstep aesthetic |
| Pitch drop FX | Drop/impact at start of drop section |
| Riser/reverse cymbal | Pre-drop build |
| Bitcrusher | Degrade and crunch bass sounds |

---

## 10. Mixdown Tips

- **Sub bass**: Must be mono; low-pass at 80–100 Hz
- **Mid bass**: Separate from sub; sits 100–500 Hz
- **Sidechain**: Bass sidechains to kick but subtly in dubstep
- **Headroom**: Leave 3–6 dB headroom for bass transients
- **Low end check**: Listen on a subwoofer and in mono
- **Loudness target**: -8 to -10 LUFS integrated

## 11. Sub-Genres at a Glance

| Sub-Genre | Key Traits |
|---|---|
| UK Dubstep | Dark, minimal, dub-influenced, spacious |
| Brostep | Aggressive, distorted, American crossover |
| Riddim | Ultra-minimal, one drop, extremely dark |
| Future Dubstep | Melodic, emotional, atmospheric |
| Tearout | Aggressive, violent bass design, industrial |
| Dark Dubstep | Ambient horror atmosphere + hard drops |
