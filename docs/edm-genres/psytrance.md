# Psytrance Production Cheat Sheet

## 1. Core Genre Characteristics

Psytrance is defined by:
- **Constant driving bassline** — the backbone of every psytrance track
- **Psychedelic textures** — alien sounds, strange FX, mind-bending modulations
- **Hypnotic repetition** — patterns that loop and evolve subtly
- **Acid lead lines** — TB-303-style squelchy, resonant leads
- **Outdoor / festival intent** — designed for open-air gatherings and altered states

The goal is transformation — music that takes the listener somewhere else.

---

## 2. Tempo

| Style | BPM |
|---|---|
| Full-on Psytrance | 142–148 |
| Dark Psytrance | 148–155 |
| Suomisaundi (Finnish) | 145–155 |
| Goa Trance (classic) | 135–145 |
| Twilight / Forest | 148–156 |
| Hitech | 170–200 |

**Recommended starting point:** 145 BPM.

---

## 3. Song Structure

Psytrance structures are cyclical and hypnotic, not dramatic.

| Section | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 32–64 bars | Build from silence; bassline enters |
| Layer 1 | 32 bars | First element added to bass |
| Layer 2 | 32 bars | Second element added |
| Mid Section | 64 bars | Full texture; subtle evolution |
| Break | 16–32 bars | Strip-down; atmospheric FX |
| Drop / Re-entry | 32–64 bars | Full energy return + new element |
| Outro | 32–64 bars | Elements strip out |

**Typical total length:** 7–10 minutes.

---

## 4. Kick Drum

### Characteristics
- Sharp, punchy, but not as sub-heavy as techno
- Very tight tail — short and precise
- Sits on top of the bassline, not below it
- Transient dominant with less body

### Pattern
```
K . . . K . . . K . . . K . . .
(Rigid 4/4; absolutely no swing)
```

### Processing
- Transient designer: maximize attack
- EQ: boost ~4 kHz for click; cut sub below 60 Hz (bassline owns that)
- Hard sidechain compression from kick to bassline

---

## 5. Bass

### Characteristics
- **The most defining element** in psytrance
- Constant 16th note riff that runs for the entire track
- Heavily detuned; distorted; modulated
- Often called the "acid bass" or "psy bass"

### Building the Psybass
1. Start with a **sawtooth** wave (or similar)
2. Heavy **distortion** (waveshaper, drive)
3. **Bandpass or low-pass filter** with resonance — aggressive
4. **Modulate filter cutoff** with an LFO or envelope
5. Tune to the root note (e.g. A at 55 Hz)
6. Run through a **saturator** again after the filter

### Example Pattern (1 bar, 16th notes at 145 BPM)
```
A  A  A  A  A  A  A  A  A  A  A  A  A  A  A  A
(same note, constant drive — variation comes from filter automation)
```

The variation comes from:
- Filter cutoff automation
- Pitch jumps on specific beats
- Occasional octave jumps
- LFO rate changes

## 6. Leads & Melody

Psytrance leads are alien, acidic, and psychedelic.

### Acid Lead (TB-303 style)
- Monophonic, resonant, filter-swept
- Notes jump in syncopated patterns
- Heavy distortion and overdrive
- Phoscyon, AcidLab Miami, or built in Serum/Sylenth1

### Main Lead
- Full-on often has a melodic lead with 2–4 bar phrases
- Often built from arpeggiator patterns
- Effects: deep reverb, long delay, chorus, flanger

### Psychedelic Textures
- Strange, detuned pads with heavy LFO modulation
- Alien sound effects, nature recordings processed beyond recognition
- Phase-shifted or flanger'd synth textures
- FX sweeps at unconventional rhythmic points

---

## 7. Chord Progressions

Psytrance often has **one or two chord centers** rather than full progressions.

| Style | Approach |
|---|---|
| Full-on | Minor keys; short 2–4 chord progression |
| Dark psy | Single sustained dark drone; no resolution |
| Goa | More melodic; 4–8 chord progressions |
| Forest | Chromatic, dissonant, atonal |

Common intervals: minor 3rd, tritone, minor 7th for tension.

---

## 8. Drums & Percussion

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| Kick | Precise, sharp, constant 4/4 |
| Clap / Snare | Beats 2 and 4; sometimes rhythmically manipulated |
| Hi-Hat | 16th note grid; slight accent variation |
| Open Hat | Sparse |
| Metallic percussion | Organic-sounding fx sounds on offbeats |
| Tribal percussion | Congas, tabla, shaker layers for depth |
| FX percussion | Alien, processed sounds as rhythmic elements |

---

## 9. FX & Psychedelic Elements

This is where psytrance differentiates itself.

| FX | Usage |
|---|---|
| Phaser | On bassline or pads for movement |
| Flanger | Sweeping texture on leads |
| Chorus | Thickening synth textures |
| Delay (ping-pong) | Psychedelic echo on leads |
| Reverb (huge) | Atmospheric textures, breakdowns |
| Pitch modulation | LFO on pitch for wobble effects |
| Ring modulator | Alien, metallic textures |
| Auto-filter | Rhythmically gated textures |
| Reverse reverb | Atmospheric pre-hit swells |
| Bitcrusher | Lo-fi, digital psychedelic textures |

---

## 10. Automation

In psytrance, evolution comes from automation, not arrangement.

- **Bassline filter**: Main expressive tool — open/close over 16–32 bar cycles
- **Lead filter**: Gradual reveal of the lead
- **FX amounts**: Gradually increase reverb size, delay feedback
- **Layered textures**: Fade in new texture layers every 32 bars
- **Pitch automation**: Occasional bass pitch sweeps for dramatic effect

---

## 11. Mixdown Tips

- **Kick and bass separation**: Critical — psybass is in the mid range; kick punches in the upper bass
- **Stereo**: Keep bassline mono or very narrow; leads can be wide
- **Frequencies**: Very dense mix — careful EQ to prevent muddiness
- **Distortion**: Creative distortion is normal — don't fight it
- **Loudness target**: -8 to -10 LUFS; must be loud for outdoor systems

## 12. Sub-Genres at a Glance

| Sub-Genre | Key Traits |
|---|---|
| Full-on | Upbeat, melodic, "happy" psychedelic |
| Dark Psytrance | Dark atmosphere, dissonant, aggressive |
| Goa Trance | Classic sound, more melodic, Indian influences |
| Forest / Twilight | Dark, organic textures, twisted |
| Suomisaundi | Quirky, Finnish humor, abstract |
| Hitech | Extremely fast (170–200 BPM), intense |
| Progressive Psy | Slower, groovier, more minimal |
